Architecture

How It Works

A production-ready architecture designed for seamless model inference, robust validation, and rapid business action.

01

Enter Details

User provides customer contract, tenure, and subscription features. Pydantic validates data boundaries at the API layer.

02

Model Analyzes

The scikit-learn pipeline applies StandardScaler & OneHotEncoder transforms, then evaluates 200 balanced decision trees.

03

Get Risk & Actions

FastAPI delivers an instant calibrated probability score and executes rule-based strategies to protect customer recurring revenue.
